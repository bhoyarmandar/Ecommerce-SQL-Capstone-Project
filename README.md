#  E-Commerce Business Performance & Data Analysis (SQL Capstone)

##  Project Overview
This project focuses on solving 30 real-world e-commerce business challenges using advanced MySQL queries. It explores customer behavior, revenue trends, product performance, logistics efficiency, and operational bottlenecks to extract actionable business insights.

---

##  Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Database Management System:** MySQL Workbench
* **SQL Concepts Used:** 
  * Multi-table Joins (`INNER JOIN`)
  * Aggregations (`SUM`, `COUNT`, `AVG`, `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`)
  * Conditional Logic & String Manipulation (`DATE_FORMAT`, `DATEDIFF`)
  * Window Functions (`DENSE_RANK()`, `SUM() OVER()`)
  * Subqueries & Derived Tables
  * Set Operations (`INTERSECT`)

---

##  Key Business Insights

1. **User Base Scale:** The platform has a registered user base of **650 customers** distributed across major urban centers.
2. **Order Volume:** A total of **2,500 orders** have been processed, indicating healthy, active platform engagement.
3. **Total Revenue:** The business has generated a cumulative lifetime revenue of **₹93,02,59,948.43**.
4. **Top VIP Customer:** **Deepak Mishra** is the highest-contributing customer, with total spending of **₹46,63,040.34**.
5. **Sales Seasonality & Trends:** **May 2024** recorded peak monthly revenue (**₹17.1 Cr**), while **February 2024** experienced the lowest seasonal dip (**₹14.03 Cr**).
6. **Cumulative Growth:** Running revenue analysis demonstrates steady, uninterrupted business expansion since launch.
7. **Payment Preferences:** **Net Banking** is the most frequently utilized payment method by customers.
8. **Logistics & Delivery:** The average delivery time for successfully fulfilled orders stands efficiently at **5.6 days**.
9. **Top-Selling Category:** The **Sports** category leads platform sales with the highest physical unit volume (**3,811 items sold**).
10. **Top Revenue Product:** The **Decathlon Yoga Mat** holds the highest individual revenue share across the entire product catalog.
11. **Geographic Distribution:** **Hyderabad** represents the city with the highest order concentration.
